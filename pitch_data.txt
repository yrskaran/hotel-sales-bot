Primary target area: Haridwar, Rishikesh and nearby Uttarakhand hotel markets

CORE PROBLEMS WE SOLVE
- Hotel WhatsApp enquiries can be missed at night or when reception is busy.

- Repetitive guest questions consume front-desk time.

- Can be customized around a hotel's own information and common guest questions.
- Can communicate naturally in Hindi, Hinglish and English; mention other languages only if they are actually configured for that hotel.
